Hammer to Fall

About Us

From the heart of Norwich's vibrant music scene rises Hammer to Fall — a Queen tribute forged in the fire, glamour, and operatic majesty of rock's greatest dynasty.

More than a band, Hammer to Fall are a spectacle — a full-throttle celebration of Queen, delivered with stadium-sized energy and meticulous musical devotion.

A Voice Fit for Royalty. At the centre of Hammer to Fall stands Chris Preece, whose electrifying performance of "Prince Ali" on ITV's The Voice UK 2020 captivated audiences nationwide.

Chris brings a vocal presence that doesn't simply echo Freddie's spirit — it commands the spotlight, soaring from tender falsetto to full-throttle rock majesty.

Together, the band transforms every venue into a night of pure Queen magic.

Meet the Band

Chris Preece - Lead Vocals

Chris brings the voice, charisma and showmanship that sit at the heart of every Hammer to Fall performance. Many will recognise him from The Voice UK, where his memorable performance of Prince Ali earned him a place on Team Olly Murs, as well as from later television appearances including StarStruck.

A graduate of Leeds Conservatoire, Chris combines professional musical training with a lifelong passion for live performance. Equally at home performing rock anthems, musical theatre favourites and Queen classics, he delivers the energy, emotion and audience connection that made Freddie Mercury one of the greatest frontmen of all time.

Shawn - Guitar

For Shawn, authenticity isn't a goal, it's an obsession. His meticulous attention to detail and relentless pursuit of the perfect Queen guitar sound have made him an integral part of the Hammer to Fall experience.

A passionate student of Brian May's playing style, Shawn has invested countless hours recreating the tones, techniques and signature harmonies that helped define Queen's music. His rig includes three vintage Vox AC30 valve amplifiers dating back to the 1960s, helping him capture the warmth, power and character of the original recordings.

From soaring solos and delicate melodies to the rich orchestral guitar arrangements that became a Brian May trademark, Shawn faithfully brings these iconic sounds to life on stage. His commitment to accuracy and musicianship ensures that audiences hear the unmistakable spirit of Queen in every performance.

David - Drums & Backing Vocals

David has been performing in bands since the 1980s and brings decades of live experience to Hammer to Fall. From original projects to cover bands, he has spent a lifetime behind the drum kit, developing the versatility and musicianship that come only from years on stage.

As well as driving the band from behind the drums, David takes on the demanding backing vocal role made famous by Roger Taylor. Combining solid rhythms with exceptional harmonies, he helps deliver the powerful sound and energy that make Queen's music so distinctive.

Simon - Bass & Backing Vocals

With more than two decades of live performance experience, Simon brings a wealth of musical knowledge to Hammer to Fall. Having played in a variety of bands over the years, he has developed a versatile style and a solid musical foundation that helps drive the band on stage.

Providing both bass guitar and backing vocals, Simon is an integral part of the rhythm section, helping to recreate the power and energy behind Queen's iconic sound. His experience, musicianship and love of live performance make him a key part of every Hammer to Fall show.

Chris - Keyboards

The first Queen album Chris owned was The Works. Decades later, the music that first captured his imagination is now the music he helps bring to audiences as a member of Hammer to Fall.

A self-taught musician and multi-instrumentalist, Chris has spent more than 30 years performing and developing his craft. His musical journey has taken him through ska, indie and rock bands, each providing its own challenges, experiences and lasting friendships. While he has enjoyed every chapter along the way, being able to perform the incredible music of Queen with Hammer to Fall remains a genuine privilege and honour.
