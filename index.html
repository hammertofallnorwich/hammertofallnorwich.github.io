About Us

A voice fit for royalty

The Legend Reborn. From the heart of Norwich's vibrant music scene rises Hammer to Fall — a Queen tribute forged in the fire, glamour, and operatic majesty of rock's greatest dynasty.

More than a band, Hammer to Fall are a spectacle — a full-throttle celebration of Queen, delivered with stadium-sized energy and meticulous musical devotion.

A Voice Fit for Royalty. At the centre of Hammer to Fall stands Chris Preece, whose electrifying performance of "Prince Ali" on ITV's The Voice UK 2020 captivated millions and earned him a standing ovation.

Chris brings a vocal presence that doesn't simply echo Freddie's spirit — it commands the spotlight, soaring from tender falsetto to full-throttle rock majesty.

Together, the band transforms every venue into a night of pure Queen magic.
